## Census and provenance

Analysis fails closed until the frozen 96-history/288-query bank, 114,048 unique scheduled packet cells, 12,096 ranking cells, all 192 scheduled reader jobs, 378 serial warm timing observations and the complete engineered maintenance replay are present. It verifies the caller's exact freeze digest, every freeze pin, mandatory analyzer/spec/reader/maintenance pins, shard digests, shard scope, exact cell identities and scheduled trial fields. The private preparation receipt must match its public freeze binding; only allowlisted numeric loading/index/storage fields are exported. Optional `--worker-receipts PRIVATE_GRID_WORK_DIRECTORY` supplies cache-reload observations from worker receipts whose exact digests match the public grid manifest. Without them, reload duration is explicitly unavailable. No host path, command, native log or authentication data enters the analysis artifact. Result input hashes and computational table hashes are recorded separately from figure export hashes. Creation time is generated automatically in UTC.

The prospective split is four development template groups (24 histories/72 queries) and twelve confirmation groups (72 histories/216 queries). The script recomputes the hash split and checks labels. Failed cells and trials stay in their scheduled denominator. Grid infrastructure faults have zero recovery/support and a recorded stage/type. Readers retain any actually observed artifact, answer and evidence gates before a later failure; frozen clean completion is a separate conjunction. Missing evidence observations cannot establish exposure. The analysis rechecks the full frozen reader score, serialized artifact hashes and any claimed passing structural/citation receipt. Missing rows are an incomplete collection error, not a completed failure row. No successful-only filtering is permitted. Numeric resource summaries identify their observed denominator and missingness; a missing duration/byte observation is not imputed as zero cost.

## Endpoints and estimators

The primary retrieval endpoint is exposure of any complete authored sufficient original-span alternative, using the union of actual source-exact, half-open Unicode character intervals. The analyzer validates source bounds/hashes and recomputes the exported support observations. Whole-original bundle recovery is a separate proxy; observed original-ID bundle coverage and candidate original-ID recovery at 8/16/32/64 are also separate. None establishes factual answer accuracy, citation entailment or minimal/exhaustive evidence. Best declared span fraction, bytes, budget omissions, traversal/missing-reference limits and stale/unverifiable derived-view selection are secondary diagnostics.

Success and coverage estimates give every group equal weight; byte and numeric resource summaries explicitly use observed-row costs and retain missing counts. Within a template group, average queries within each history, then histories within the group. Report development and confirmation independently. Resample whole template groups with replacement, retaining all their families/queries and paired arms. Use 2,000 replicates, seed 1013 and descriptive 2.5/97.5 percentile intervals; for confirmation there are only twelve authored clusters. A shared bootstrap index matrix is used for matched contrasts. These intervals describe this scaffolded fictional bank, not a representative corpus/population or an independently authored human sample. Raw denominators and per-group means accompany aggregate estimates. No population p-values or significance labels are produced.

Ranker contrasts compare each ranker with body BM25 at the same base packer, bytes and corpus size. Packer contrasts compare each scheduled extended-ranker packer with whole-record packing at the same ranker, bytes and corpus size. Candidate contrasts compare each ranker with body BM25 at the same size and k. Match exact `(family_id, task_id)` identities, refuse unmatched arms, retain ties and improvements/regressions and their IDs. Every scheduled comparison is exported, including negative results. A selected maximum among 21 rankers and many packers is exploratory and cannot be called a confirmed unique winner; intervals are not adjusted for selection or multiplicity.

The 24 matched direct histories cover twelve groups, two histories per group: 19 authored-ready and five authored-hold tasks. False holds use authored-ready tasks as denominator; justified holds require complete task on an authored-hold task. It does not infer natural desktop usability.

A submission receipt is a delivery proxy, not measured comprehension/provider intake. Trials without a verified support bundle are reported as not reached, not silently removed from success denominators.
