## Review depth and executable checks

One reviewer read every source and query in all sixteen initial variant-one mechanisms, then read all sixteen broadened hold mechanisms and eight added navigation/view source patterns. The final resolving authorities were read in every mechanism at variants two, four and six. One complete long workshop background was also read. Repeated variants were checked using independently extracted source parameters and exact source slices, rather than described as 288 independent semantic adjudications.

The private audit independently recomputes historical, current and resolved follow-up values from source text: minimum ceilings, stock arithmetic, denominator exclusions, exhaustive component sets, ordered SOP insertions, exact token concatenation, current custody, registered aliases, closed-window thresholds and ordered map pages. It checks all 96 variants against the corresponding typed answers. Known facts on hold tasks are checked separately from the unresolved field and reason. The sixteen hold mechanisms were reviewed for actual source absence, incompatible applicability or genuinely coequal conflicting claims; a newer capture timestamp does not settle a conflict.

Each ready follow-up has a separately retained, signed, scope-matching governing original received at 21:00 UTC. Initial availability/conflict packets are explicitly captured at 18:00 UTC and remain retained. Conflict adjudications explicitly supersede both coequal originals; missing attachments become real source references; new matching authority preserves incompatible Annex or class-specific evidence. Each final original states every requested known and resolved fact, so its singleton evidence alternative is genuinely sufficient. Ready follow-ups include both positive and negative decisions where applicable. Their `hold_reason` is JSON null; hold follow-ups retain a reason code and a null unresolved factual field. Status comes from authority and support, not a global timestamp rule.

Every declared evidence alternative uses retained originals, with valid half-open Unicode character offsets into unchanged bodies. The exposed slices contain the operative blocks; in some historical alternatives only the separately appended expiry sentence is omitted, while the retained operative block already establishes the queried historical interval. Exact string/set/ordered-list contracts and typed gold examples pass structural validation. UTF-8 roundtrips preserve source text. Source presence alone is not accepted as sufficient passage exposure. Alternatives remain authored sufficient sets, not exhaustive or proven minimal evidence.

The graph census verifies absent references against explicit declarations, rather than treating every missing file as an intentionally absent dependency. The 24 added live hubs cite current source revisions and pass the actual packer's `declared_current` check despite incorrect prose. They are retained as derived views with no authority to amend originals. This demonstrates a metadata-consistency limitation; no semantic validator was smuggled into the gate.

## Corrections and broadening before outcomes

The initial candidate passed a narrower review, but remained unfrozen. Four substantive review issues were corrected: an alternative source had to establish that an override **cannot** waive a ceiling rather than merely that no waiver had been issued; shipping-list presence had to be distinguished from unavailable enrollment; an ordered SOP amendment needed its original baseline in the primary evidence set; and an exact canonical service name needed its original registry. The author separately corrected an inaccurate old-retention age assertion without changing the governing hold result.

The initial candidate also reused one eleven-record scaffold and one missing-source hold pattern. Before outcomes, it was broadened with varied record counts, permuted role IDs, source-location chains, current-ledger/wrong-prose views, actual absent graph edges and three distinct hold mechanisms. A subsequent review aligned the declared scopes of Annex and purchase/input-specific originals. A shipping source and an Annex enrollment source were separated to avoid assigning misleading single-scope metadata to mixed authority claims. Natural source titles replace generic unresolved-status titles. These are pre-outcome fixture corrections and additions, not post-result score changes.

A final pre-freeze broadening permuted task-role IDs and array order, replaced status-cued follow-up requests with neutral end-of-day requests, and supplied 48 actual resolving originals. Earlier missing/scoped/conflicting records were preserved, while their availability statements were qualified by the initial packet time. All task IDs and array positions now contain both ready and hold cases. A final contract-only correction explicitly includes JSON null in the `hold_reason` enumeration, matching the declared string-or-null type. No new scored retrieval or reader outcomes were inspected during these amendments or this audit.

## What this review cannot establish

The reviewer is an LLM, and no inter-rater agreement or human adjudication was measured. Parameter checks depend on the reviewed authored mechanisms and do not understand arbitrary new prose. Several repeated template structures remain, including a shared parameter-variant schedule for follow-up status; permutations do not make the variants independent. The wrong-view/missing-edge/navigation patterns are deliberately engineered. The bank is useful for controlled failure analysis, with clustered uncertainty and an explicit selection-risk ledger; it does not establish population accuracy, natural-vault usability, biological plausibility or a universal filing convention. External benchmark transfer and fresh-session completion remain separate evidence requirements.
