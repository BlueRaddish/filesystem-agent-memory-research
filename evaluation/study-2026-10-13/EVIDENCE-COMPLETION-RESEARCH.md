# Evidence completion, state and retention: post-analysis extension

Reviewed **October 15, 2026 UTC**, after the main analysis and its public release.
These four newly catalogued preprints did not select or modify the frozen
methods. This is selected-section primary-source review, without experimental
reproduction, implementation validation or a new measured outcome. The
[source ledger](evidence-completion-sources.json) records exact versions, review
depth, repository access limits and two additional biological discovery records
whose full-text access failed. The historical 125-record bibliography remains
unchanged; catalog entries are not automatically independent studies.

## EC01: compose evidence and reach past the initial pool

BFR selects complementary records, then accumulates unseen text/entity/session
hits over a fixed store. Its offline sufficiency judges receive the reference
answer; the rate definition excludes uncertain/missing labels, although the
reported pilot had no uncertain labels. The running policy stops on
budget exhaustion, without an oracle for sufficient evidence. At matched three
calls, fixed text completion beats the tested learned residual on Gold Hit,
which remains an any-support-record proxy. This supports a controlled comparison
of access schedules rather than assuming a learned controller must win.
[EC01](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09348v1)

## EC02: recover the complement of the current agent state

MSS-Complement targets evidence still missing at a captured decision. Budget
definitions change the comparison: its Complete-MSS advantage is 11.6 points at
five units but 0.6 at a 6,144-token cap. On AMA-208, a smaller answer prompt
coexists with more online tokens: 33.57K versus 13.95K including controller work.
The accuracy difference's clustered interval includes zero. The source-discovery
cohort shares 434 states with Test500, so it is not 500 additional independent
cases. [EC02](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20050v1)

## EC03: distinguish source units from graph scoring

EPGM separately evaluates provenance-aligned units and a residual graph scorer.
The candidate-view comparison also changes eligible fields, preventing a pure
segmentation attribution. Graph gains vary by query and budget; direct recall's
interval includes zero, and very tight budgets can hurt partial coverage.
Observable execution edges do not certify semantic dependency. Human review
consulted model judgments rather than independently reannotating blind; the
offline synthetic traces and limited model-judged answers do not establish live
agent success. [EC03](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.25913v1)

## EC04: evaluate retention before revealing future questions

The value-model study separates future-query-blind retention from query-time
similarity. Its experiment keeps 30% of turns, uses four active factors while
three remain inert, and measures retained gold rather than downstream answers.
Twenty resampled splits reuse 479 usable cases; they are not twenty independent
collections. Our application would first test indexing or promotion priority
while retaining original evidence, instead of treating a retention proxy as
permission to delete it. [EC04](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.12945v1)

## Atomic follow-ups, all unexecuted

The following are our proposed transfers, not findings of these papers or new
results of the primary study. Use independently authored histories, sealed
future questions and matched candidate/call/source-byte budgets. Report complete
qualifier exposure and actual continuation separately.

| Switch | Matched contrast | Failure that would reject the idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requirement-set selection | Select complementary intact source units versus independent record relevance | Redundant items displace a necessary exception despite equal budgets |
| Observed-state conditioning | Provide an exact already-seen span ledger versus query alone | Novelty suppresses an essential earlier source or fails to recover missing support |
| Provenance conditioning | Keep candidate units/eligible fields fixed; add real versus shuffled/no edges | Gains disappear under matched units, or wrong edges dominate a correct lexical route |
| Query-blind retention priority | Rank before future questions are revealed; preserve full originals as a control | Apparent gains require future-query leakage or remove rarely used governing evidence |

These factors can be crossed after individual checks. Keep a fixed text-paging
baseline alongside more elaborate controllers. A self-assessed sufficiency stop
also needs an explicit false-stop test: a fluent declaration cannot certify an
unseen exception. A fixed stopping budget is an honest operating limit, not a
completeness proof.

Our completed study's passage bottleneck, metadata-dependent authority ceiling
and maintenance edge failures provide concrete cases for those tests. Its
authority-pair result is an intrinsic span proxy; that policy was not one of the
five executed direct-reader arms. The literature extension neither upgrades that
proxy into task success nor establishes a natural-PARA or biological mechanism.

## What is available to inspect

The official repository snapshots add implementation leads, not reproduced
results. BFR's MIT-licensed release describes a retrieval pipeline and a small
synthetic example, with optional encoder fallbacks; its example is not the
paper benchmark. SERBench releases a benchmark interface, starter and data,
with held-out certificates private. Its software license and annotation grant
have separate scopes, and upstream excerpts retain their original terms.
GraphMemory documents reproduction commands and separate data/model downloads;
the inspected tree contains no license-named file. The paper-linked BorgeAgent
URL returned HTTP 404 to an anonymous request. That access result does not
establish why it was unavailable. Commit pins and inspection limits are in the
ledger. No implementation, dataset or model was run for this extension.
