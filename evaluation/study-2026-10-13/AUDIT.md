## Numeric review scope

The independent numeric route did not import or rerun the analyzer. It consumed the already audited raw grid and reader labels and independently reconstructed all 792 packet cells, seven raw packet metrics per cell, all 504 ranker / 384 packer / 396 corpus contrasts, all 336 candidate cells and 336 candidate contrasts, 192 compact reader rows and ten arm summaries, direct/discovery paired comparisons and the factorial interaction, 378 timing observations, and 12,800 maintenance events. It checked the exact raw query identities, improvements, ties, regressions and regression-ID lists rather than checking only aggregate rates. A separate raw-integer extraction matched all 792 packet-cell counts directly from 114,048 rows.

Query means were recomputed within histories, then histories within authored templates. Confirmation cells use 72 histories / 216 queries in 12 groups; development uses 24 / 72 in four groups. Reader direct/oracle arms use 24 cases across 12 groups, while discovery uses 12 cases/groups. Costs retain observed and missing denominators, with null costs not converted to zero. Complete task is the conjunction of the five underlying gates; clean completion separately applies the frozen protocol.

The independent bootstrap checks used stdlib seeded draws for 152 fixed source-selected summaries, including both splits of the main passage/extended-packer cells, candidate-k32 cells, corpus contrasts and reader contrasts. Each used 2,000 whole-template replicates and interpolated descriptive percentile endpoints. Other reconstructed summaries were checked for raw group means, denominators, point estimates and interval bounds. Floating comparisons used relative tolerance 1e-11 / absolute tolerance 1e-12; counts and hashes were exact. None was silently normalized.

Original-ID bundle labels were checked for endpoint ordering rather than freshly derived from gold. Ready/hold labels and earliest sufficient-support semantics were inherited from the accepted audit/analysis; clock values were independently bound to eligible verified delivery records. There was no fresh bank/gold grading, semantic-case/key reading, native-trace interpretation, model/provider call, raw-audit rerun or frozen-analysis rerun in this numeric review.

Two earlier private numeric-checker attempts failed on harness schema assumptions: optional reader fields differ by block, and scheduled action caps differ from observed event counts. Their exact sources, commands, streams and exit codes remain retained. Corrected checks validate each frozen job and independently count observed events. These were checker failures, not study failures. The final consuming check passed; failed attempts are not erased from the record.
