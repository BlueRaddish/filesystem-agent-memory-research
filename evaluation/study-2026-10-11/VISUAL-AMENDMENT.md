# Post-collection label and legend amendment

The fifteen scientific figures were generated by the prospectively pinned
`analyze.py`. Visual inspection found overlapping annotations or obstructing
legends in figures 05, 07, 08 and 15. The separate [render_labels.py](render_labels.py)
repairs presentation after collection. It does not change the frozen analyzer,
experiment, primary outcomes, statistical summaries or measured point locations.

- Figure 05 moves the category legend below the axis labels.
- Figure 07 moves coincident byte annotations into a per-panel table and numbers
  the engine color key. Its dotted frontier and uncertainty intervals are unchanged.
- Figure 08 replaces overlapping engine annotations with a color key in unused
  plot space. Measured points remain coincident where the observations coincide.
- Figure 15 moves both legends below the axes so they do not conceal bars.

The helper imports the original plotting function and intercepts figure saving.
It closes the eleven unaffected figures without rewriting their twenty-two files.
For each amended figure it asserts that numeric lines, scatter offsets, path
vertices, patches and axis limits are identical before and after the label change.
All computational fields in `analysis.json` are compared unchanged; only four
figure-registry entries receive new image hashes. The independent collection audit
does not claim to audit display layout.

The helper binds the complete computational-table digest rather than demanding
identical original SVG timestamps/IDs on a fresh analyzer run. Its four revised
SVG exports use a fixed ID salt and omit the creation timestamp. Original and
revised byte hashes certify the observed files, independently of that reproducible
numeric-output check.

[visual-amendment.json](visual-amendment.json) records the original and revised
analysis hashes, renderer hash, unchanged computational-table hash, original and
revised eight image hashes, numeric-artist fingerprints and a runnable self-check.
The original complete analysis had SHA-256
`3182f66a9d6ed56f9c5d1a9e75e820bbd5a9dc01742447204ed278def412f527`.
This amendment is explicitly post-collection; it is not part of prospective
hypothesis or metric selection.

To reproduce the final display, first run the frozen analyzer as documented in
the report, then `python render_labels.py --self-check` and
`python render_labels.py --render` from this study directory. No weights or reader
calls are needed for the display step. No generated HTML or raster synthesis is
used; PNG and SVG retain the scientific plotting source.
